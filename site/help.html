For players

  • Open the link your captain sent. There's no account, app or sign-in.
  • Pick a game, then tap an inning to see who plays where. All shows the whole game as a table, and Season shows innings played, sat, infield and outfield across games with a result.
  • The field shows a first name and last initial. A ? after a name means that player answered Maybe.
  • "The lineup isn't set yet" means nobody has said they're coming. Check back closer to game day.
  • Share lineup image makes a picture of the whole game to send to a group chat.
  • The Anonymous suggestion box, if your captain has it on, sends a note to your captain without your name.

Captains: getting in

Open the team link with #captain on the end, then tap Edit beside the team name and sign in with Google. After that, the Edit button shows on that phone or computer by itself. The setup page also lists a Captain tools link for each of your teams.

While editing, Player view shows the page exactly as players see it, without signing out. Edit takes you back.

The tabs are Lineup, RSVPs, Games, Roster and Team. Everything saves as you go: there's no Save button.

Games and the season

  • On a new team, Add a season adds every game at once from a first date and a number of weekly games.
  • Add a game adds the next game a week after the latest one, at the same time and place. Change anything on its card. Each field saves when you leave it.
  • For a playoff, give the game a Label, like Semifinal. Put the score in Result, like Won 9-5. A game counts toward season totals once it has a result.
  • Archive hides a game from players, for a rainout for example. Archived games are listed at the bottom with Unarchive.
  • Tapping a game also makes it the one Lineup and RSVPs show.

RSVPs

  • Mark each player Yes, Maybe or No as replies come in. Tap the same answer again to clear it.
  • Only Yes and Maybe players are in the game. Someone with no reply stays off the field and out of the kicking order until they answer. A No keeps their positions in case they come back.
  • If your team doesn't collect replies, Mark … with no reply as Yes puts everyone in at once.
  • The count at the top warns when fewer than 8 are playing. × takes a player out of this game. Add from the roster brings in a sub or a late arrival, who kicks last.

Building the lineup

  • Pick an inning. Tap a position, then pick a player. They swap with whoever was there, and after you fill an open spot the next open one comes up.
  • To place someone who's sitting, tap them under Sitting, then tap a position.
  • In the kicking order, tap a kicker, then the kicker whose spot they should take.
  • You can also drag anyone. On a phone, hold their name for a moment until it lifts, then drag.
  • From inning 2 on, Copy inning 1 here (or whichever inning comes before) copies the previous inning so you only change a few spots.
  • On a game whose field is still empty, Copy lineup from the last game brings over its positions and kicking order for everyone playing in both.
  • Positions given to two players, or left open while someone sits, are flagged in red for you. Players just see "open".

Sharing the lineup

  • Send players the team link from Team. It shows the lineup and nothing else, and nobody needs to sign in.
  • Share, beside Undo and Redo, makes the same lineup image players can make: positions down the side, innings across, who sits and the kicking order.

Undo, Redo and the sheet's history

Undo reverses your last change and keeps going back. Redo puts back what Undo took away, until you make a new change. Both last for this visit.

For anything older, the sheet keeps its own history of every change and who made it. Open the sheet (Team has a link), then choose File, Version history, See version history, or press Ctrl+Alt+Shift+H (⌘+Option+Shift+H on a Mac).

Roster and Team

  • Roster lists everyone. Tap a name to change it or their preferred and won't-play positions, kicking style, pronouns and notes. Renaming someone renames them in every game. Removing someone asks first and leaves them in games already set up.
  • Team holds the team name, season label, icon, colors (pick one or type a hex code like #2b6cb0), stripe style and thickness, font and the suggestion box. Changes show right away.
  • Suggestions land in the Comments tab of your sheet.

Co-captains

Open the sheet, choose Share and add your co-captain as an Editor. Then send them the co-captain link from Team. The first time, they pick the team sheet in Google's file picker.

Editing the sheet by hand

You never have to, but the sheet works on its own too:

  • Roster: one player per row, with the positions they prefer and won't play, notes, pronouns and kicking style.
  • Schedule: each game's number, date, time, location, opponent, label, result, and Yes in Archived to hide it.
  • Game … Lineup tabs: a player per row in kicking order, their reply under RSVP, and a position code (P, C, 1B, 2B, 3B, SS, LF, LC, CF, RC, RF) in each inning column. The Open spots row lists positions nobody covers.
  • Team Config (hidden): the team's look. View, then Hidden sheets, brings it back.

Edits in the sheet show up on the page within about half a minute.
