Privacy

Last updated October 18, 2026

The Kickball field view lets a team captain share a lineup from their own Google Sheet with players. It is a small free project run by Duncan Wood. This page explains what it does with your information.

If you are a player

Opening a team link does not require an account. The page does not use cookies, advertising or analytics. If you send a suggestion through the suggestion box, the text you type is added to the captain's sheet. It is not tied to your name or any other identifier.

If you are a captain

When you sign in with Google, the app asks for one permission: access to the files it creates for you or that you pick yourself (Google's drive.file permission). It cannot see any other file in your Google Drive.

To keep the team link working, the service stores:

  • the ID of the one spreadsheet you created or picked,
  • an encrypted Google refresh token that lets the service read that spreadsheet and write suggestions into its Comments tab,
  • the date you connected it.

It does not store your name, email address or profile information.

What players can see

The team link shows only the team settings and the lineup tabs: names, positions, kicking order, and each game's date, time, location, opponent and notes. Roster notes, the Comments tab and every other tab stay private.

How information is used and shared

Your information is used only to show your team's lineup and to deliver suggestions to your sheet. It is not sold, shared with advertisers or used for anything else. The service runs on Cloudflare, and sheet data is read from Google. The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

You can disconnect at any time by removing the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which makes the stored token stop working. To have the stored team record deleted, open an issue at github.com/Duncan-Wood/kickball/issues and include your team link. Your spreadsheet stays in your Drive and is yours to keep or delete.

Changes

If this changes, the date above changes with it.
