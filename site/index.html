Kickball Field View

Your kickball lineup, drawn on a field. Players open one link on their phone and see who plays where in every inning, and the kicking order. You keep the lineup in a Google Sheet.

Set up your team

Got a link from your captain? Just open it. There is nothing to sign in to.

What your players see

  • A field for every inning, with each player at their position
  • The kicking order, who is sitting, and the game's time, place and opponent
  • A season view of how many innings everyone has played, in the infield, the outfield and on the bench
  • An anonymous suggestion box, if you want one

No account and no sign-in. It updates by itself within seconds of your edits.

What you edit

Your lineup lives in a Google Sheet in your own Drive, so you can change it from the Sheets app on the sideline or from a laptop. Type a position in each inning, and strike through the name of anyone who can't make it. The field view follows.

Your roster notes and anything else in the sheet stay private. Players only see the lineup tabs.

How it works for captains

  1. Sign in with Google. It takes a minute and you only do it once.
  2. Create your team sheet. You get a ready-made sheet in your Drive with a sample roster and game to edit.
  3. Send your team the link. It opens the field view and cannot change anything.
