What the service does

It shows a lineup kept in a Google Sheet to the people who have the team link, and lets people who can edit that sheet change it from the team page. The sheet belongs to the captain who created or picked it, and stays in their Google Drive.

Captains

If you set up a team, you decide who gets the team link and who can edit the sheet. Only add names and details of people who expect to be on your team's page, and take the link down with Stop sharing when you no longer need it. Do not use the service for anything unlawful, to harass anyone, or to store sensitive information in the sheet. Anyone with the link can see the lineup tabs, including game notes.
